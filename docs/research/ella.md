## Facts verified
- **Setting.** About 1,040 m, Badulla District, Uva Province. Cooler than the lowlands. The Ella
  Gap gives views across the southern plains.
- **Little Adam’s Peak.** 1,141 m (Sri Lanka Tourism, Wikipedia). Start off the Passara road near
  98 Acres. About 1.5–2 km each way, 45 minutes to 1 hour; easy to moderate, with steps near the top.
- **Ella Rock.** No road. The usual route runs along the railway about 2.5 km towards Kital Ella,
  then a path near a bodhi tree before the station. 3–4 h return (sources say 2–4 h). Touts may
  misdirect walkers. A working line.
- **Nine Arches Bridge.**
  - Between Ella and Demodara stations; nine spans; about 91 m long, 24 m high; stone, brick
    and cement.
  - Archaeological protected monument. No barriers; trains cross several times a day; tracks
    walk from Ella station about 20–45 min.
- **Demodara loop.** The line circles a hill and passes under itself through a tunnel beneath the
  station.
- **Train.**
  - Ella is on the Main Line. Pattipola is the highest station, at about 1,898 m; the page says
    "about 1,900 m".
  - Online reservations open up to 30 days ahead. Classes listed include observation, AC saloon,
    2nd and 3rd.
  - Nanu Oya–Ella about 2–2.5 h by timetable (blogs); the page says 2.5–3 h to allow for delays.
  - Nanu Oya–Badulla reopened 20 June 2026. Kandy–Nanu Oya was still not through-running in
    October 2026 (blog).
- **Safety.** A Russian tourist died after falling from the Podi Menike in February 2025. Other
  serious injuries were reported in 2024 and 2026 from leaning out or standing on footboards.
- **Day trips.**
  - Ravana Ella: about 25 m, about 6 km from Ella on the Wellawaya road, visible from the road;
    linked by legend to Ravana and a nearby cave.
  - Lipton’s Seat: above the Dambatenne factory (built 1890 by Lipton), about 9 km north-east of
    Haputale; best early, before mist (often by about 10 am).
  - Diyaluma: about 220 m, near Koslanda.
  - Horton Plains via Ohiya is 38 km from Haputale (Sri Lanka Tourism).
- **Climate** (Badulla normals):
  - Wettest October–December (242–286 mm); January 184 mm.
  - Driest June–August (52–72 mm); February–March 88–102 mm.
- **Journeys (estimates).**
  - Nuwara Eliya–Ella about 60 km.
  - Ella–Tissamaharama about 89 km, 2–3 h.
  - Ella–Arugam Bay about 131 km, roughly 2.5–4 h.
  - Colombo–Ella by car 5–6 h (GoAsia); the page says 6–7 h from the airport.
  - Ella–Udawalawe: no direct source found; estimated at 2.5–3.5 h via Wellawaya and
    Thanamalwila.

## Uncertain or conflicting
- Bridge date: 1919 (Wikipedia) vs 1921 (several guides, CCF listing). The page says "around a
  century ago". The steel-shortage and P. K. Appuhamy stories are folklore without records; not used.
- Bridge height: 24 m (Wikipedia) vs 30 m (some guides). The page uses 24 m.
- Demodara loop date (1921 vs 1923) and length; the uniqueness claim. Not used.
- Lipton’s Seat elevation: 1,431 m to about 2,000 m in different sources (1,970 m most common).
  Not stated on the page.
- Diyaluma height: 220 m (most sources) vs 171 m. The page says "about 220 m".
- Ella Rock duration: 2–4 h in different sources.
- Ella–Udawalawe time and Ella–Arugam Bay distance are not well sourced. They are given as ranges.
- Nine Arches train crossing times differ between sources, so the page gives no times.
- Rail status in October 2026 rests on one blog. Needs official confirmation.
- visituva.lk: the footer names the Uva Provincial Council’s Ministry of Tourism, but the site
  does not state that it is official. It is listed in `sources:` and flagged in `verify:`.

## Experience copy suggestions
**ella-little-adams-peak** (Little Adam’s Peak hike)
- `where`: not needed (the walk starts in Ella).
- `details`: A 1,141 m hill named for its likeness to Adam’s Peak, reached on a gentle path
  through a tea estate off the Passara road, with steps near the top. Allow 45 minutes to an
  hour each way, and go early for clearer views and fewer people.
- Note: the category is `adventure`; `nature` may suit an easy walk better.

**ella-nine-arches-bridge** (Nine Arches Bridge)
- `where`: Demodara, about 2 km from Ella town
- `details`: A curving railway viaduct of stone, brick and cement on nine arches, about 91 m long
  and 24 m high, built around a century ago. Walk about 30–45 minutes along the tracks from Ella
  station or take a tuk-tuk to a nearby path; it is a working line with no barriers, and train
  times change, so check locally.
