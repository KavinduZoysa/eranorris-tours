## Facts verified
- **Location.** About 35 km east of Galle; Colombo about 146–154 km by road. Fisheries harbour in the
  sheltered eastern corner of Weligama Bay, just west of the village; whale boats leave from it.
- **Beach features.**
  - Parrot Rock: outcrop just off the main beach, reached by wading at low tide; dangerous at high
    tide and in monsoon swell.
  - Coconut Tree Hill: lateritic headland with palms; privately owned (about 400 perches, two
    owners); a fence put up in January 2021 without approval had to be removed; reopened February
    2021. Access arrangements are not formalised in any source found.
  - Secret Beach: small cove on the western side, via Harbour Road and a steep final path on foot.
- **Seasons.** SW monsoon May–September brings swell and strong currents to the south and west
  coasts; NE monsoon period (about November–April) is the calm season on the south coast;
  October–November inter-monsoon is often stormy. Most whale-watching trips stop in the SW monsoon.
- **Safety.** Coast Guard lifesaving post at Mirissa; five foreigners (incl. a 6-year-old) rescued
  from currents on 23 August 2023; Coast Guard urges caution on western and southern beaches during
  the SW monsoon. 16 lifesaving posts nationally (2023).
- **Nearby.** Weligama about 5 km west, popular for learning to surf. Dondra Head lighthouse, 49 m,
  first lit 1890, about 6 km south-east of Matara, at the southernmost point. Tangalle about 45 km east.
- **Journeys** (page uses wider ranges than Rome2Rio):
  - Airport about 175 km, 2.5–3 h.
  - Colombo by road 2–2.5 h; rail about 3 h.
  - Galle 45 min–1 h.
  - Yala 2.5–3 h; Udawalawe 2–2.5 h; Ella 3.5–4.5 h.

## Uncertain or conflicting
- Coconut Tree Hill: current access and any entry fee are not confirmed by any recent source. The
  page says it is private land and arrangements can change.
- Turtles near Mirissa's rocks (e.g. "Turtle Bay"): single travel-blog source. The page says only
  that turtles are "sometimes seen" and gives no location.
- Mirissa–Ella times vary widely by route (via Udawalawe or via the coast and Wellawaya). The page
  says 3.5–4.5 h.
- Busiest weeks: "December to March" is a general pattern from travel guides, not an official figure.
