## Facts verified
- **Status.** National park declared 30 June 1972; 308 km²; centred on the Udawalawe reservoir on
  the Walawe River; protects the catchment and shelters animals displaced by the reservoir. On the
  boundary of the wet and dry zones, between Sabaragamuwa and Uva provinces.
- **Landscape.** Mostly flat, open grassland and scrub with dead trees left from earlier farming;
  greener with temporary pools after rain.
- **Elephants.** Estimates: about 250 resident (Wikipedia), about 500, about 600 (Rough Guides).
  The page says "from about 250 to several hundred". Seen year-round.
- **Other wildlife.** Water buffalo, spotted deer, sambar, wild boar, golden jackal, mongoose, toque
  macaque, grey langur, mugger crocodile; leopard and sloth bear present but rarely seen.
- **Birds.** About 184 species (33 migratory); birds of prey, painted storks, spot-billed pelicans.
- **Climate.** About 1,500 mm a year, mainly October–January and March–May.
- **Safaris.** Gates open about 6 am; vehicles out by about 6 pm; drives about 3–4 h; early morning
  and late afternoon recommended. Permits via DWC at the gate or online.
- **Elephant Transit Home.**
  - Established 1995 by the DWC.
  - Orphaned and abandoned calves raised with minimal human contact; released at about 5 years.
  - Six released 17 July 2025 (26th release); 187 released since 1998 (AFP); 57 at the facility then.
  - Visitors watch feedings from a viewing area; no touching; little to see between feeds.
- **Roadside feeding.** Elephants have waited at the park fence along the reservoir road for food
  from passing vehicles; DWC built a second fence around 2011–2012; feeding wild animals is illegal
  under the Fauna and Flora Protection Ordinance (FT 2019 commentary).
- **Journeys** (page uses longer, wider ranges than Rome2Rio):
  - Airport 3.5–4.5 h; Ella 2–2.5 h; Tissamaharama/Yala about 2 h.
  - Mirissa 2–2.5 h; Galle 2.5–3 h.

## Uncertain or conflicting
- Elephant numbers vary widely by source (250–600).
- Elephant Transit Home feeding times conflict: 10.30, 14.30, 18.30 (Audley; Wanderlog); 9.00, 12.00,
  15.00, 18.00 (DTH Travel); others say morning and evening only. The page says "set feeding times …
  check them locally". No official DWC page found.
- Release totals: 187 (AFP, July 2025) vs "over 100" (Sun Siyam, 2026). The page says "well over a
  hundred".
- Born Free Foundation involvement is claimed by two tour operators only; not mentioned on the page.
- The current state of roadside feeding and the fence (sources are 2011–2019). The page says "for years".
- No source confirms an annual closure for Udawalawe; one operator states there is none. The page
  says "no regular annual closure", and notes that the department can close parks at short notice.
- Whether only registered jeeps may enter (as in Yala Block I) is not confirmed for Udawalawe. The
  page says visitors "tour the park in safari jeeps" and makes no rule claim.
