## Facts verified
- **Setting.**
  - Ampara District, Eastern Province; about 320 km from Colombo, 117 km south of Batticaloa
    and about 4 km south of Pottuvil.
  - The main settlement (Ullae) is predominantly Muslim, with Tamil and Sinhalese communities
    in the area.
  - Fishing was traditionally the main livelihood.
- **Tsunami.** Much of the bay and Pottuvil was destroyed in 2004.
- **Surf.**
  - Season April–October (Sri Lanka Tourism, two pages); offshore winds in the south-west
    monsoon months; busiest June–August.
  - Main Point: reef, right-hander, can be big and hollow; Baby Point (inside) is a lesson spot.
  - Pottuvil Point: boulders then sand, intermediate-friendly; murky lagoon water and
    crocodiles in the wet season.
  - Elephant Rock: soft boulder point for beginners to lower intermediates; elephants and
    lagoon crocodiles nearby.
  - Peanut Farm: learners on small days; surf-school hub May–August.
  - Okanda: about 23–28 km south; strong intermediates and above; rips near the headland.
  - Arugam Bay has hosted Sri Lanka’s international surf competitions since 2004.
- **Wildlife.**
  - Elephants move between Lahugala and Kumana.
  - Lahugala Kitulana: about 1,554 ha around three tanks, about 16 km inland from Pottuvil;
    herds of up to about 150 between June and August.
  - Kumana: about 35,664 ha; formerly Yala East (renamed 2006); adjoins Yala; bird sanctuary
    since 1938.
  - Tens of thousands of birds gather at the Kumana swamp April–July.
  - Permits and a guide are obtained at the Okanda office. About 40–44 km from Arugam Bay via
    Panama and Okanda.
- **Temples.**
  - Muhudu Maha Vihara: Pottuvil beach; legend of Queen Viharamahadevi; three granite statues.
  - Kudumbigala: forest hermitage near Panama; 200+ caves; stupa on a rock reached by
    rock-cut steps.
  - Magul Maha Vihara: near Lahugala.
- **Off-season.** North-east monsoon rain and onshore winds roughly November–March; many
  businesses close.
- **Security alert.** On 23 October 2024 the US Embassy restricted travel to Arugam Bay over a
  possible threat to Israeli tourists; the UK and others issued warnings. Arrests followed. The
  US lifted the restriction on 13 November 2024, and Israel lowered its warning the next day.
- **Journeys.**
  - Airport/Colombo–Arugam Bay about 320 km, about 6.5–7.5 h (Daytrip). The page says 7–8 h to
    allow for airport traffic and stops.
  - Ella–Arugam Bay about 120–140 km via Wellawaya and Monaragala, about 3–3.5 h.
  - Trincomalee–Arugam Bay about 250 km, about 4–5 h 15 min.

## Uncertain or conflicting
- Sri Lanka Tourism places Arugam Bay in Batticaloa district; it is in Ampara District.
- Sri Lanka Tourism gives the "best time" as May–November on one line and the surf season as
  April–October on another. The page uses April–October (the design text and the season stated
  on both official pages).
- Sri Lanka Tourism calls Pottuvil Point "the Peanut Farm"; surf guides treat them as separate
  breaks (Pottuvil Point north of Pottuvil, Peanut Farm south of Arugam Bay). The page treats
  them as separate.
- Whiskey Point: older guides call it beginner-friendly; The Surf Atlas says erosion has
  damaged its sandbars. The page leaves it out.
- Peak months: July–August, July–September or June–August, depending on the source. The page
  says "around July and August".
- Ella–Arugam Bay: estimates from 2 h 18 min to 6 h 28 min (the longer ones use other routes).
  The page says "about 3–4 hours".
- Yala (Tissamaharama)–Arugam Bay: no direct source. It is built from Tissa–Monaragala (81 km,
  about 1 h 20 min) plus Monaragala–Arugam Bay (about 2 h). The page says "about 3.5–4.5 hours".
- Lahugala access rules (walking, vehicles inside the park) differ between sources. The page
  says only that herds gather at the tanks.
- No standing annual closure was found for Kumana; closures have been event-driven (drought,
  rain, and 1985–2003 for security).

### Facts verified
- No current government advisory names Arugam Bay (UK FCDO, Sept 2026). The October 2024 alerts were
  lifted in November 2024. Security forces maintain a presence (Daily Mirror, Mar 2026).
- Surf season about April–October; October–November shoulder days (Surf Atlas); Whiskey Point no longer
  reliable; Lighthouse is a quieter break to the north.
- Crocodiles near several breaks and lagoons; elephants on the roads south of town.
- Kumana: permit and guide at Okanda; jeep entry; campsites closed for drought from 2 Sep 2026; park
  closed for rain in late Nov 2025; foot-pilgrimage gate opened 10 Jul 2026 (June in 2025).
- Kumana from Arugam Bay: about 40–44 km; about 1–1.5 hours (Surf Atlas: about 1.5 hours).
