## Topics researched
History and royal patronage of the cave temple; UNESCO status; the five caves and what is in
each; statue and mural counts; the climb; tickets and who sells them; dress, shoes,
photography and monkeys; the modern Golden Temple building; nearby sights (Ibbankatuwa,

## Facts verified
- **Status and history.**
  - UNESCO 1991; in continuous religious use (UNESCO, CCF).
  - Valagamba (Vattagamani Abhaya) used the caves in exile and later made them a temple, 1st
    century BC (CCF, Wikipedia, Rough Guides).
  - Nissanka Malla gilded statues in about 1190 (Wikipedia, CCF).
  - The Kandyan kings restored and repainted the caves in the 18th century (CCF, Wikipedia).
  - It is an Archaeological Protected Monument (1957).
- **Rock.** About 160 m above the plain (CCF, Wikipedia).
- **Caves** (CCF, with Wikipedia):
  - Cave 1 has a 14 m reclining Buddha cut from the rock, and Ananda.
  - Cave 2 is the largest, about 52 m wide and 23 m deep. It has 56 Buddha statues (16 standing,
    40 seated), statues of Valagamba and Nissanka Malla, and a dripping spring said to heal.
  - Cave 3 is Kandyan-period, with about 50 Buddha images and King Kirti Sri Rajasinha.
  - Cave 4 has a seated Buddha under a makara torana and a small dagoba.
  - Cave 5 is the most recent, with reclining images and Hindu deities.
- **Totals.** More than 150 statues; about 2,100 m² of murals (UNESCO, Wikipedia, CCF).
- **Visiting.**
  - Tickets are bought at the base before climbing; there is no counter at the top (two
    independent reviews plus Wikivoyage).
  - Shoulders and knees must be covered or entry may be refused.
  - No flash.
  - Monkeys are around.
  - The climb is about 10 min (Wikivoyage) to 30 min (blog).
  - Sigiriya is visible from the top (CCF).
- **Around.**
  - Ibbankatuwa: about 3–5 km south-west on the Kurunegala road, roughly 2,700 years old
    (700–400 BC), with granite slab tombs, pottery and beads; a 20–30 min visit.
  - Popham's Arboretum: started in 1963 by Sam Popham, a dry-zone forest on the Kandalama road
    with trails and night walks (slender loris).
  - Dambulla is the island's main vegetable distribution centre (Wikipedia).
  - Nalanda Gedige: 8th–10th century stone shrine in Pallava style with Buddhist use, moved in
    the 1980s for the Bowatenne reservoir.
  - Aukana: 5th century, 11.84 m (over 14 m with pedestal), facing Kala Wewa.
  - Spice gardens: free entry, buying expected, high prices (Rough Guides).
- **Journeys.**
  - 148 km from Colombo and 72 km from Kandy (Wikipedia).
  - Sigiriya about 17–20 km; Polonnaruwa 66–67 km (Wikivoyage); Trincomalee about 108 km
    (Wanderlog).

## Uncertain or conflicting
- **Who sells tickets.**
  - The CCF ticket page lists the cave temple with online booking, but the CCF price list shows
    only the Dambulla Museum.
  - Third-party prices vary: LKR 2,000, 3,000 or about USD 8–10.
  - 2017 reports said the CCF would take over ticketing, followed by a revenue-sharing agreement
    between the temple and the Archaeology Department.
  - The page says "the counter at the bottom" and links the CCF page. Confirm the current
    arrangement before publishing.
- **Ticket counter location.** Wikivoyage says it is not on the golden-Buddha side (go round to
  the side road with parking). The page does not say which side.
- **Hours.** CCF 7 am–5 pm vs Wikivoyage 07:00–19:00. Not stated on the page.
- **Statue counts.** 157 (UNESCO nomination) vs 153 + 3 + 4 = 160 (Wikipedia) vs "over 150"
  (CCF). The page says "more than 150".
- **Murals.** UNESCO gives 2,100 m² for the whole site, while CCF attributes 2,100 m² to Cave 2.
  The page uses the site-wide figure.
- **Reclining Buddha.** 14 m (CCF, Wikipedia) vs 15 m (elsewhere on Wikipedia). The page says 14 m.
- **Golden Temple building.** The date (often given as about 2000), the statue's height and
  funding are not verified. The page states none of them.
- **Shoe deposit.** A small fee is reported by reviewers. The page does not mention it.
- **Aluvihare.** "First written down of the Buddhist scriptures" is the standard account, but
  this research has not checked it against a second written source. Check, or soften.
- **Nalanda Gedige.** Its distance from Dambulla is not given in sources; it is about 20 km
  north of Aluvihare. The page gives no distance.
