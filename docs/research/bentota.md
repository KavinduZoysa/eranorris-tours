house, late-1960s national holiday resort, Bawa’s Bentota Beach Hotel); the beach and sea

## Facts verified
- **Setting.** About 65 km south of Colombo and 56 km north of Galle on the A2. Bentota is on the
  south bank of the river mouth (Galle District, Southern Province); Aluthgama is on the north
  bank (Kalutara District, Western Province). About 10 km from the Southern Expressway’s
  Welipenna exit. Most trains stop at Aluthgama (about 2–2.5 km); Bentota is a small halt.
- **History.**
  - Portuguese fort at the river mouth in the 17th century.
  - Dutch rest house for officers between Colombo and Galle, later used by the British.
  - A 1938 motoring guide called it the halfway point in the old coaching days.
  - Designated the first national holiday resort in the late 1960s.
  - Geoffrey Bawa’s Bentota Beach Hotel was built on the rest-house site, about 1967–1969 with
    work into 1970. It is now Cinnamon Bentota Beach.
- **Beach and sea.** Long, wide beach; calm from about November to April (north-east monsoon
  months); rough with red flags more often in the south-west monsoon (about May–October; sources
  vary on the exact months). Currents are reported near the river mouth.
- **River.** Mangroves and islets; kingfishers, herons, cormorants; water monitors; crocodiles
  sometimes. Trips 1–3 hours; morning and late afternoon recommended. One independent account
  describes an operator offering a baby crocodile for photos. Hence the page’s "don’t handle or
  feed" advice.
- **Water sports.** Sri Lanka Tourism lists water-skiing on the river and estuary, windsurfing
  and more. Travel guides say jet skis, banana boats and wakeboarding run on the sheltered
  river and lagoon.
- **Lunuganga.** Bought in 1948. Run by the Geoffrey Bawa Trust; guided tours only, and outside
  guides are not permitted. Steep steps and uneven paths.
- **Brief Garden.** Bevis Bawa’s garden near Kalawila, about 10 km inland; a former rubber estate,
  with many sculptures. Open to the public since about 1969 (one source).
- **Kosgoda.** About 11 km south; several privately run hatcheries.
- **Galapata Raja Maha Vihara.** Old temple with medieval inscriptions, carvings, pillars and ponds.
- **Journeys.**
  - Airport–Bentota about 1.5–2.5 h (two guides).
  - Colombo Fort–Aluthgama train about 1 h–1 h 30 min.
  - Bentota–Hikkaduwa about 37–39 km, about 40–45 min.
  - Bentota–Galle about 57–72 km, about 1 h or more.
  - Kandy–Bentota about 176 km, about 2 h 40 min–3 h+.

## Uncertain or conflicting
- Brief Garden dates: Bevis Bawa acquired it in 1929 (several sources) or 1949 (one). Garden
  size 4.9 vs 20 acres. The page gives neither.
- Wikipedia’s Bentota article confuses Lunuganga with Brief Garden and calls Kosgoda "a
  community project with the Wildlife Department". The page does not use that claim; Sri
  Lanka Tourism describes the hatcheries as private.
- Monsoon months: travel sources give May–October, June–August or May–September for the rough
  season. The page says "about May to October".
- Bentota–Galle: 57–72 km depending on route. The page says "about 60 km" and "about 1–1.5 hours".
- Kandy–Bentota: 2 h 40 min (Rome2Rio) to 3.5 h (calculators). The page says "about 3–4 hours"
  to allow for Colombo traffic.
- Mirissa "around 2 hours" via the expressway is an estimate from route distance (about 120 km).

## Experience copy suggestions
For `src/data/experiences.mjs`. The river safari and water sports are in Bentota itself, so no `where`.
On 2026-11-05 the villa site's Madu River tour moved here from Hikkaduwa and was merged into
`bentota-river-safari` ("River safari and mask museum"), combining the river-safari copy below
with the Madu Ganga copy in docs/research/hikkaduwa.md. Balapitiya is about 20 km south of
Bentota (same figure as the guide). The two rivers are different places, so the card names both.

- **bentota-river-safari**
  - `details`: Boats head upstream from the river mouth between mangroves and small islands, usually for one to three hours. Kingfishers, herons and cormorants are common, water monitors often seen and crocodiles sometimes, but nothing is guaranteed. Mornings and late afternoons are cooler and better for birds.
- **bentota-water-sports**
  - `details`: Jet skis, water-skiing, wakeboarding, banana boats and windsurfing run mostly on the sheltered river and lagoon near the mouth, away from the open sea. The sea is calmer from about November to April. Operators vary, so check that life jackets are provided and fit.
