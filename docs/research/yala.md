## Facts verified
- **Status.** Reserve from 23 March 1900; national park 1 March 1938. Total about 979 km² in blocks
  (Block I 14,101 ha, II 9,931 ha, III 40,775 ha, IV 26,418 ha, V 6,656 ha); the DWC's 2026
  announcement refers to Zones I–VI.
- **Climate.** Semi-arid; 500–775 mm a year, mostly in the NE monsoon. Hottest around April; driest
  and most stressed August–September.
- **Leopards.** Block I: at least one leopard per km² (Mongabay 2026), among the highest densities
  recorded; many habituated to vehicles.
- **Other wildlife.** 44 mammal species; elephants (numbers vary seasonally); sloth bear (seen mainly
  in palu season, about May–July); spotted deer, sambar, wild boar, water buffalo, grey langur;
  mugger and saltwater crocodiles; about 215 bird species.
- **Entry rules.**
  - Since January 2024 only registered, licensed jeeps (552 at the time) may enter Block I.
  - Drivers trained and advised to switch engines off near animals; fines or suspension for
    violations; strict time slots for entry and exit.
  - Government proposes a cap of about 250–300 jeeps per session and GPS tracking (not yet in force).
  - Permits from DWC at the gate or online; daily permit limits.
  - Gates open about 6 am; vehicles out by about 6 pm (multiple operators; no official page found).
  - Visitors may leave vehicles only at designated places (e.g. Patanangala).
- **Crowding.** About 380,000–390,000 visitors to Block I in January–June 2025; "leopard jams".
- **Closures.** Blocks I and II usually close for several weeks between September and October;
  2026: 14 September–15 October; dates vary by year (see sources).
- **Cultural sites.** Sithulpawwa rock monastery (traditionally 2nd century BC) reached through the
  park; Patanangala memorial to the 2004 tsunami (about 47 people died at the bungalow site).
- **Nearby.** Kataragama: multi-faith pilgrimage town (Buddhist, Hindu, Muslim); annual Esala festival
  in about June–August (2025: 26 June–10 July). Bundala: Ramsar wetland (1990), migratory waterbirds,
  greater flamingos in some years.
- **Journeys** (page uses longer, wider ranges than Rome2Rio):
  - Airport about 290 km, 4–5 h.
  - Mattala (HRI) about 27–30 km from Tissa, under an hour; few flights (140,614 passengers in 2025).
  - Ella 2.5–3 h; Udawalawe about 2 h; Mirissa 2.5–3 h; Arugam Bay 3–4 h.

## Uncertain or conflicting
- Closure dates change every year and are announced at short notice. The page gives 2026 dates as an
  example only. Re-check each year.
- Gate times: operator sources only; no official DWC page.
- "Block" vs "zone" naming: the DWC now uses "zones" in announcements; blocks are still widely used.
  The page uses both.
- Which other zones are open on a given day, and from which gates: unclear; the page names Galge and
  Lunugamvehera generally.
- Speed limit in the park: no current official figure found; not stated on the page.
- Migrant bird season: general (about October–April); no single official source.
- Journey times are estimates; owner's own figures preferred.
