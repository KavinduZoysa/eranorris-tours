# Research record — Experience: Minneriya elephant safari

Researched 2026-11-06 and 2026-11-07 for `src/content/experiences/minneriya-elephant-safari.md`.
Internal record, not published. Experience id: `sigiriya-elephant-safari` (proposed in
`.cache/audit/cultural-triangle.json`; not yet in `src/data/experiences.mjs`).

## Why this page, and why under Sigiriya
- Clear search intent ("Minneriya safari", "elephant gathering Sri Lanka", "Kaudulla or
  Minneriya"), and the topic is distinct from the Sigiriya and Polonnaruwa guides, which give it
  one paragraph each.
- The experience is listed under `sigiriya` because Sigiriya (2 nights in the design) is the
  usual base, Habarana and Sigiriya are where most safari jeeps are hired, and all three areas
  are about 30–60 minutes away. Polonnaruwa (Minneriya's entrance about 25–30 km away) is the
  other realistic base; a second card under `polonnaruwa` is proposed as optional in the JSON.

## Topics researched
The Gathering (cause, season, peak, numbers); its collapse after 2018–2019 and recovery; the
2025 Court of Appeal undertaking; the three areas (Minneriya, Kaudulla, Hurulu), their status and
managing bodies; how drivers choose a park on the day; time of day; tickets and online permits;
the 2025 Kaudulla ticket fraud; planned unified ticketing; jeep numbers and crowding; elephants on
roads; feeding law; other wildlife; Cyclone Ditwah closures; captive elephant rides.

## Facts verified
- Cause of the Gathering: grass on the exposed tank bed as the water falls in the dry season
  (Daily FT 2025; Sunday Times 2024; former DWC DG in the Sigiriya record).
- Season and peak: about July–October with a peak in August–September (Sunday Times 2024,
  operators); WNPS gives May–October with the peak around September (Daily FT 2025). The page
  says "about July to October … peak around August and September" and "in some years it begins
  earlier, as the water starts to fall from about May".
- Numbers: over 400 at peak (WNPS/Daily FT; 402 in Sept 2017); about 400 (Sunday Times 2024);
  Wikipedia 150–200 typical with reports to 700. The page says "more than 400 elephants have
  been counted" and "a few dozen … or a great many more".
- Collapse and recovery: 20 in Sept 2021 (Daily FT); recovered in 2024 and 2025 (Pilapitiya,
  Sunday Times). Court undertaking late 2025 (Daily FT).
- Herds move to Kaudulla around September (Wikipedia; operator reviews 2026).
- Hurulu: Forest Department eco park inside a 1977 biosphere reserve (Daily Mirror; UNESCO).
- 400–600 jeeps a day in Minneriya and Kaudulla (Newsfirst 2026; the article does not attribute
  the figure to a named official, so the page says "have been reported").
- Unified Minneriya–Kaudulla ticketing planned (Newsfirst 2026).
- Kaudulla ticket fraud and action against 10 jeeps (Daily Mirror; Gold FM).
- Feeding is prohibited (SLTDA safari guideline 2024; Daily FT 2019 on the FFPO); 30 km/h limit,
  marked routes and right of way for animals (SLTDA guideline).
- "Kaudulla is often quieter": 81,112 visitors in 2025 vs 155,146 at Minneriya (SLTDA Year in
  Review 2025); an operator blog says the same.
- Afternoon drives are the norm and elephants emerge late in the day (two operator sources and
  the Sunday Times 2024 article's description of evening gatherings; no official source).

## Uncertain or conflicting
- **Season start.** May (WNPS) vs July (most sources). Hurulu's best months: July–September
  (Daily Mirror 2021) vs December–January (operators). The page avoids months for Hurulu.
- **When the releases stopped hurting the Gathering.** Daily FT says unseasonal releases ran
  2018–2023; Pilapitiya says water has been managed without cutting off grazing since 2022. The
  page says "from about 2019" and "back in strength in 2024 and 2025".
- **Online permits for Minneriya and Kaudulla.** The ePermit page names no parks; travel guides
  list Minneriya (and some Kaudulla). The page says "where the park is listed". Check.
- **Park hours and fees.** Not stated (DWC publishes no clear current page; ePermit shows
  charges). Reports of about 6 am–6 pm; not used.
- **Private vehicles.** Whether private cars may enter Minneriya was not confirmed; the page
  only says visits are by safari jeep.
- **Kaudulla's tank.** Wikipedia says it is one of 16 tanks built by Mahasen and was rebuilt in
  1959; not used on the page.
- **2026 Gathering.** No official count found. Visitor reviews from August–September 2026 report
  herds at Minneriya and Kaudulla (one of more than 60 elephants at the tank, one of about 20
  with about 50 jeeps). The page does not describe 2026.
- **New entrance near Sigiriya–Pekkulam and the Batu Oya exit.** Under consideration only; not
  on the page.
